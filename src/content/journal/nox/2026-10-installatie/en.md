Nox is in. It's my first aquarium at a client's: a tank of 110 by 68 by 65
centimetres, 420 litres, in an office in Leuven.

The name comes from the Latin word for night. The client likes to work late
here, and the aquarium follows that rhythm: the light only comes on at 11 in
the morning and goes off at half past eleven at night. After that, a soft
night light stays on until 2 a.m.

The substrate is MA-Gen 2.1. The filter is internal and built entirely
around biological filtration, with a mechanical prefilter in front. There is
no CO₂.

The water is still cloudy: the haze has to clear first. The plants need time
to settle in too. They were grown emersed, above water, and are now making
the switch to their submersed form. That takes time.

The turbulent first month has begun. For now, only bacteria live in the
tank. Snails and fish will follow later, once the ecosystem is ready for
them.
