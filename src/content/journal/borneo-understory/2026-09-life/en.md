The first inhabitants are in: blue Neocaridina shrimp and a small group of
chili rasboras. The fish are still adjusting and look a little nervous.

I also gave the tank a firm pruning. The Cryptocoryne are growing on well in
the meantime, and the rest of the planting is visibly taking root. The scape
now feels fuller than it did in August.
