After about a month and a half, the ecosystem is starting to settle. All the
plants have taken off well, and a balance is forming.

There is still some green thread algae, but the tank is starting to look
better and more natural.

The Chili Rasboras are still a little nervous. I see the shrimp more and
more often, especially in the hour before the light goes off.
