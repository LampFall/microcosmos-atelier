Four months after setup, the system is really starting to work. The Monstera
and Pothos with their roots in the water draw nutrients straight from it —
exactly the intention. That keeps the water clear without much intervention.

Below the surface the Cryptocoryne have recovered after the usual melt phase.
The fish are in and spreading nicely across the water column.
