Since the forest closed in last year, little changed in how the tank looked.
Now a sand slide has broken that quiet balance: the sand has shifted forward
and built up into a clear sand front in the foreground.

The shift shows in the planting too. The Echinodorus has declined, while the
Ambulia has spread considerably and now fills a large part of the background.

The next step is to bring more visual calm into the scape, so the sand front
and the planting hold each other in balance instead of crowding each other
out.
