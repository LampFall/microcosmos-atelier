I've changed the lighting. I'm now testing a Chihiros lamp with a little
extra red in the spectrum. The light is softer and warmer than before.

The balance is still settling. The nutrient shortages seem to be levelling
off a little, but we're not there yet.
Compared with April, the Echinodorus leaves are fresh green again.

One of the Apistogrammas has trouble with its swim bladder. It eats well,
but struggles to swim around.

The bristlenose has grown considerably. At feeding time it claims its food.

I've also reworked the hardscape a little. The extra wood gives the water a
lovely colour, but there's still room for improvement, especially on the
right-hand side.
