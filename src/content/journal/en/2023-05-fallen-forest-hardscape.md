The largest tank so far: two metres long, a thousand litres. Today I built the
hardscape around the idea of a fallen tree — the open spaces between the
branches matter just as much as the wood itself.

No water in the tank yet. First I want to be sure the structure holds up, and
that there are enough hiding places and sightlines for what will later become
the tetras and corydoras.
